Chairman's Message

Dear fellow Members, Players, Parents, and Supporters,

Championship Week: One Club, One Community, One Goal 💚🤍

As we continue our journey towards building a stronger One Club, this week presents a fantastic opportunity to bring that vision to life. Across football, hurling, camogie and LGFA, six Ballincollig teams will take to the field in some of the biggest games of their season. It is a week that showcases the strength, talent and commitment that exists across our club. More importantly, it is a chance for us all to come together and support each other as one Ballincollig family.

Tonight (Fri) U16 Camogie — Championship fixture vs Midleton (away)
Sunday U14 Hurlers — Championship Final in Blarney
Sunday LGFA J1 — County Semi-Final at home in Ballincollig
Sunday U14 Camogie — Championship fixture vs Watergrasshill (away)
Monday Premier 1 Minor Footballers — County Final at Páirc Uí Rinn 🏆 Chasing an incredible three-in-a-row!
Thursday U16 Footballers — County Final in Bishopstown 🏆

The excitement gets underway this evening as our Under 16 Camogie team travel to Midleton. Sunday promises to be a huge day, with our U14 Hurlers in a Championship Final in Blarney, our LGFA J1 team battling for a place in the county final at home, and our U14 Camogie team in action in Watergrasshill.

The excitement continues on Monday as our Premier 1 Minor Footballers take centre stage in Páirc Uí Rinn for their County Final, chasing an incredible three-in-a-row of Premier 1 County titles. Opportunities to achieve something this special do not come around often. This outstanding group has earned its place on the biggest stage through dedication, sacrifice and relentless hard work, and now has the chance to write another memorable chapter in Ballincollig history. Let's fill Páirc Uí Rinn with green and white!

The week concludes on Thursday when our Under 16 Footballers take to the field in their County Final in Bishopstown. After a tremendous season, they too stand on the brink of something special.

Behind every one of these teams are countless training sessions, early mornings, late evenings, dedicated coaches, supportive families and players who proudly wear the Ballincollig jersey. They have all made sacrifices to reach these occasions and now deserve the full backing of the club.

Let's create a championship atmosphere that reflects everything that is great about our club. Let's show what One Club means in action.

Six Teams. Six Huge Occasions. One Ballincollig. 💚🤍

Le meas,
Tom Dorgan
Chairman, Ballincollig GAA Club
